# VANTA — Game Design Foundation

## Gameplay loop
Explore → drive/travel → discover locations → complete missions → earn resources → upgrade equipment/vehicles → unlock new areas.

## World
A large seamless world divided into streamable cells. Roads, settlements, wilderness and points of interest should be authored as independent streaming regions.

## Player
Third-person controller with acceleration/deceleration, slope handling, grounded/airborne states, camera collision and animation-ready movement data.

## Vehicles
Wheel/suspension simulation, traction, braking, steering, surface response and configurable drivetrain parameters. Damage is designed as a later extension.

## Android
Touch movement, camera swipe, contextual interaction, vehicle controls and scalable HUD. Bluetooth/controller input can be added without changing gameplay systems.
